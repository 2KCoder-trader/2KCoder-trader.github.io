Common questions

How do I cut a shape?

Start on an empty spot and swipe straight through the shape. Swiping on a piece picks it up instead, so start your swipe just outside it.

How do I get 3 stars?

Clear the board using the fewest possible cuts. Hold the top bar during a level, or hold a level on the map, to see what each star needs.

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap Hint to see your best next move. +1 Cut gives you an extra cut, Undo takes back a move, and Hammer smashes every block in a 3x3 area.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Purchases go through Apple. Restart the game first. If it's still missing, contact us with the date of purchase, or request a refund through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
