FAQ

What kind of game is Slice & Fill?

A block puzzle game for iPhone. Unlike classic block puzzles where you only place pieces, you cut shapes into smaller pieces first, then fill the board with them.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free to play with optional ads and optional in-app purchases. No account is needed.

Can I play offline?

Yes. All levels work without an internet connection.

How do I get 3 stars?

Clear the board in the fewest possible cuts. Hold the top bar during a level to see exactly what each star needs.

What games is it like?

If you enjoy block puzzles, tangram-style shape puzzles, or level-based logic games with 3-star ratings, Slice & Fill adds a new twist: you decide where to cut each shape.

When can I download it?

Slice & Fill is in final testing and coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
