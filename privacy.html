Slice & Fill

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Slice & Fill ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. The short version: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name or email, and the gameplay data we collect isn't tied to who you are.

Gameplay data we collect

To balance level difficulty and fix problems, the game sends anonymous play data to our server:

  • A random player ID created on your phone the first time you open the game (not your Apple ID, name or any device identifier)
  • Your device model, iOS version, language/region setting and screen size
  • What happens in a level: which level, the cuts and moves you make and their timing, boosters used, buttons tapped, the result and score
  • The game's build number

This data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) and is only used to improve the game. We don't sell it and don't use it for advertising. A copy is also kept on your phone; deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards. Google may collect information such as your IP address, device information, ad interactions and, only if you allow it in Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change tracking permission anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the game to remove all data stored on your phone and stop any further data from being sent.
  • To ask about, or request deletion of, play data already sent, contact us through the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the support page.
